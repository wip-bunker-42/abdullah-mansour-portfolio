## The opportunity was already in the search results {#opportunity}

Zone10 had relied on relationships and offline marketing. People were looking for the services it offered, but competitors were the businesses they could find in Google Search.

The task was to establish a channel that could capture relevant demand, present the offer clearly and help prospective customers start a conversation with sales. The targeting, pages and measurement all needed to be built.

## I separated the demand by what customers needed {#audiences}

I studied the services and used Google Keyword Planner to research search demand, distinguishing residential needs from commercial projects.

A homeowner considering a villa or a large apartment needs a different message from a business fitting out a clinic, restaurant, café or exhibition space. Those differences shaped the keyword groups, ads and page content.

I also used the Google Ads API and Python to automate parts of keyword research and examine thousands of variations. That widened the research and helped surface less obvious opportunities, with final selection based on intent and commercial relevance.

## The conversion action needed to fit the buying process {#landing-pages}

Interior design and fit-out decisions involve a discussion about the space, requirements and budget. I made calls and WhatsApp the primary page actions, giving visitors a direct route to the sales team.

I wrote, designed and built the landing pages, aligning the offer with the service and audience. The mobile version kept those contact options easy to reach as visitors considered the proposition.

## An early measurement window informed the expansion {#measurement}

For the campaign shown, **February 1–18, 2026** produced:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Clicks | 131 |
| Recorded conversion actions | 33 |
| Conversion rate | 25.19% |
| Cost per conversion action | US$12.42 |


Conversions in this report track interactions with the contact buttons. Costs for this reporting window are presented as USD equivalents using **US$1 = EGP 47.01** on **February 18, 2026**, rounded to two decimal places. This reference rate is the midpoint of that day’s published buying and selling rates, rounded to two decimals. That evidence supported developing more specific campaigns, messages and pages for different audience segments. The project expanded to four campaigns and four landing pages.

## Search terms kept the work connected to actual demand {#optimization}

I reviewed search-term reports continuously, adding negative keywords and adjusting bids, budgets, ads and page elements according to traffic relevance and conversion performance.

Broad, phrase and exact match each had a place where appropriate. Their use followed the intent of the search and the evidence from the campaign.

## What the business gained {#outcome}

Zone10 had a working Google Search acquisition channel, supported by dedicated messaging, pages and a measurement setup for further improvement.

Across December 2025–April 2026, I managed more than US$4,000 in ad spend across four campaigns. More than {{metric:zone10.contacts.value}} people contacted the business through the project.

My responsibility covered the connected journey. When performance pointed to an issue, I could work on the keyword, ad, page or measurement, then follow the decision through the same acquisition setup.
