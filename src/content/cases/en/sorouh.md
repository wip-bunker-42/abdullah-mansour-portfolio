## The first task was to map commercially relevant demand {#demand}

The company started without a website, tracking or an organized digital presence.

I studied its services and coverage, then researched hundreds of service-and-location combinations using Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, competitor research and live search results.

I assessed intent, commercial relevance, local specificity and the opportunity to create useful, distinct content. Those judgments shaped what the site would contain.

## I selected sixteen local service pages from 170 possible combinations {#architecture}

Seventeen services across ten cities could produce 170 service-and-city pages. I built **sixteen**, where demand, intent and content justified a dedicated page.

The wider site contained **seventy-one indexable pages**:


Each keyword group with a shared intent had a designated page. I created a keyword ownership registry to make that relationship explicit and help prevent pages competing for the same searches.

The Dammam building-contractor page shows the approach in practice: a specific service, a local context and a clear route to call or WhatsApp.

## I directed AI agents to deliver the site and its SEO foundations {#delivery}

I translated the research into page, content and measurement requirements, then directed AI agents through implementation with Astro, TypeScript, Sanity and Cloudflare. I defined the architecture, guided execution, reviewed the output and connected the work to checks before publication.

Delivery included crawlable HTML, canonicals, sitemaps, structured data, internal links, redirects, image optimization, indexability controls and Arabic right-to-left handling.

I also developed automated checks for titles, descriptions, H1s, links, orphan pages, schema, sitemaps and redirects, alongside mobile and desktop rendering and accessibility. These checks brought quality review into the process before changes reached production.

## Measurement followed the contact action back to its page {#measurement}

I configured Search Console, GA4, Google Tag Manager and Bing Webmaster Tools, with Ahrefs and Microsoft Clarity supporting analysis.

A shared `contact_click` event recorded the contact method, CTA placement and landing page, and was connected to GA4 Key Events.

That gave the company a way to assess which pages prompted contact, alongside their search visibility and clicks.

## A delivered site, with its first search activity {#outcome}

From **August 14–September 4, 2026**, Search Console recorded **26 impressions and 7 organic clicks**.

![{{evidence:sorouh-gsc.alt}}](/media/evidence/sorouh/03-gsc-performance.png)

*Initial search activity after launch: 26 impressions and 7 clicks in the reporting window shown.*

The handover included a published site, content mapped to search intent, contact measurement, technical quality checks and the first signs of search visibility after launch.

The central decision was what to build. Giving each page a commercial purpose brought research, content and implementation into the same plan, with a structure the company could continue developing.
