## Timing was part of the editorial proposition {#timing}

Studying broadcasters and independent creators revealed a useful opening: the time between a story breaking and an explanation reaching the audience.

Video production could consume much of that window. I developed a format that allowed analysis to reach viewers earlier, informed by the audience’s response to direct presentation and competitors’ live formats.

The topic and angle mattered. So did how quickly a considered explanation could be produced and distributed.

## I built the production process around the opportunity {#production}

I chose a filming format that required less editing and developed an AI-assisted tool to control the phone from my computer, pause and resume recording, and transfer the footage.

I then built a zero-cost publishing and scheduling tool for distribution across {{metric:elkhabar.platforms.value}} platforms, without opening each platform separately or paying for a social media management service. Time saved in filming would have had less value if the upload and publishing process remained a bottleneck.

On active publishing days, I averaged six videos. Publishing automation saved more than {{metric:elkhabar.saved.value}} hours of daily work, helping me run the project alongside client engagements.

AI also supported news collection, analysis, topic selection and writing. I remained responsible for editorial choices, the angle and what was published.

Growth subsequently relied primarily on organic distribution. I used retention, watch time, shares, non-follower reach and follower acquisition to develop the topics, openings and formats.

## A request to follow was getting in the way {#retention}

In one experiment, I placed a follow-and-engage request immediately after the opening hook. My assumption was that curiosity about the story would keep people watching through it.

Retention showed viewers leaving at the interruption. I moved the request later in the video, or to the end, after the explanation had earned their attention.

That became part of how I tested the structure, alongside the opening, pace and treatment of the topic.

One **2:19 YouTube video** recorded:

- **1:47 average view duration.**
- **92% of viewers still watching at 30 seconds.**
- **78.1% choosing to stay and watch.**

## What this project demonstrates {#outcome}

I identified an audience opportunity, built a content proposition around it, operated the production and distribution, and changed decisions when the response challenged my assumptions.

It also made the relationship between content and operations tangible. A faster format and less manual publishing gave me more room to research and test, while helping the explanation reach the audience when the story mattered.
