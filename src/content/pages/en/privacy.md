## Messages and email {#messages}

When you contact me, your name, email, optional company and message are sent through Cloudflare to my Gmail inbox so I can respond. This site does not store a separate message database. Correspondence is managed in that inbox. To request deletion, email {{profile:email}}.

## Website analytics {#analytics}

Cloudflare Web Analytics helps me understand visits and site performance. Form fields and message contents are not sent to analytics. Your theme choice is saved locally in your browser when storage is available.

## Infrastructure {#infrastructure}

Cloudflare hosts and delivers the website and processes contact submissions. Google provides the destination email inbox. Their services process the information needed to provide those functions.
