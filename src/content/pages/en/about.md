## The thinking behind AIMansour. {#brand}

The name combines **AI + Mansour**: artificial intelligence and my name. As my work developed, AI became central to how I delivered marketing, helping me research, analyze, build what a project needed and reduce repetitive effort.

The name reflects that additional capacity within my marketing practice. I assess the tools through the work they make possible, the time they release and the problems they help me solve.

## Three decisions from the work {#decisions}

For Zone10, deciding which searches to exclude was part of the budget decision. I built negative-keyword lists for free resources, courses, job searches and software, and refined them through search-term reports to keep the campaigns focused on demand for the company’s services.

For Sorouh, seventeen services across ten cities offered {{metric:sorouh.possibilities.value}} possible page combinations. I selected {{metric:sorouh.local.value}} service-and-city pages with a reason to exist: relevant demand, a distinct intent and useful content.

For Elkhabar Bewodooh, I tried asking viewers to follow immediately after a video’s opening. Retention showed that the interruption was losing people. I moved the request later, after the explanation had delivered value.

These are the conversations I enjoy having about my work: the evidence behind a choice, the alternatives and what changed after putting it into practice.
